# Traveller Pay

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

International travellers scanning Indian merchant UPI QR codes. Current users are testing the development app.

## Product Purpose

Connect and sign in with MetaMask, review a Tempo pathUSD test balance, scan a merchant QR, and enter or review the INR amount.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Tempo Moderato and pathUSD test funds only. INR settlement is simulated and must be labeled. Payment submission is currently unavailable. Never fabricate balances or transaction history. MetaMask owns wallet keys and signature approval.

## Brand Commitments

Traveller Pay. Clear, approachable payment language. Android-first native Expo interface.
