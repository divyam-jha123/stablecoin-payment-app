# Traveller Pay

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

International travellers scanning Indian merchant UPI QR codes. Current users are testing the development app.

## Product Purpose

Connect and sign in with MetaMask, review a Tempo pathUSD test balance, scan a merchant or TravelPe demo QR, enter or review the INR amount, and share a demo Receive QR.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Tempo Moderato and pathUSD test funds only. INR settlement and TravelPe Receive requests are simulated and must be labeled. Receive requests may select USDC, USDT, or pathUSD as demo receiving currencies; no token payout is made. The scanner's payment panel uses clearly labeled mock balances and confirmation simulates the payment without moving funds. Payment submission is currently unavailable. Never fabricate connected-wallet balances or transaction history. MetaMask owns wallet keys and signature approval.

## Brand Commitments

Traveller Pay. Clear, approachable payment language. Android-first native Expo interface.
